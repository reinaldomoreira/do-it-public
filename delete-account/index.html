Delete your do-it account

You can delete your account and all of its data yourself, inside the app, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open do-it and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account → Delete account.
  3. Type the confirmation word and tap Delete forever. You need an internet connection.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, profile and preferences, on every device.
  • Your tasks, notes, labels, reminders, saved views, places, attachments and comments.
  • Sections and notebooks you own that have no other editor, with everything in them.
  • Your Alexa link, other integrations and registered devices.

Sections and notebooks you own that have an editor are handed over to their oldest editor, together with the tasks and notes you created in them, so your collaborators do not lose their shared work. Your membership in other people's shared sections is removed.

Deletion is immediate and permanent. Copies in routine backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Cannot open the app?

Ask us to delete the account for you. Use the contact details in the privacy policy and write from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, so we can verify it is yours. We complete verified requests within 30 days.
